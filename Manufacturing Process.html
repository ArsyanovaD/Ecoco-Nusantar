Product Quality & Specifications

Our briquettes are crafted from 100% natural coconut shells, completely free from chemical additives, binders, or artificial fillers.

  • Shisha / Hookah: 2.5 to 3 hours
  • BBQ Charcoal: 4 to 5 hours
  • Shisha Charcoal: Very low ash content of 2.5% to 3% with 4–6% moisture, burning down to a fine, clean white-to-light-grey ash.
  • BBQ Charcoal: Ash content of 5% to 8% with 5–7% moisture, with high caloric density and excellent ventilation design for heavy grilling.

Our briquettes feature low moisture content (under 6% to 7%) and high fixed carbon content of around 70% to 80%, ensuring maximum heat output and minimal smoke.
