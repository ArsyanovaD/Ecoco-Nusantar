| WHO WE ARE

From Nature to Global Standards.

SPECIALIZED INDONESIA CHARCOAL MANUFACTURER

Ecoco Nusantara is a leading Indonesian charcoal manufacturer, specialized in the export of premium charcoal briquettes. We operate a modern integrated production facility to obtain and maintain wood-charcoal briquettes for BBQ, delivering high-quality products that meet international standards.

With integrated production facilities and strategic access to premium raw material sourcing from Sumatra and Sulawesi, we ensure a stable supply chain, consistent quality control, and efficient throughput at scale.

Beyond supplying premium charcoal, we provide OEM and private label solutions with flexible sizes, shapes, and packaging to match your brand. At Ecoco Nusantara, we are dedicated to building long-term partnerships by delivering reliable products, consistent quality, and trusted export solutions for the global market.

| WHAT WE OFFER

Elevating Indonesian Charcoal for Global Markets

COMMITTED TO STRENGTH, INNOVATION, AND CARE

At Ecoco Nusantara, we extract quality directly from the source through our specialized production facilities in Indonesia. Dedicated exclusively to manufacturing high-performance charcoal briquettes, our integrated operations ensure reliable production capacity, consistent product quality, and a stable supply chain capable of meeting large-scale global demand.

Every batch undergoes a rigorous quality control process, starting from raw material selection, moisture content testing, and burn efficiency, ensuring consistent performance and stability across every shipment. This comprehensive system guarantees high heat content, low ash content, and clean, long-lasting performance.

Beyond manufacturing, we provide efficient worldwide distribution through dedicated port access and full containerized shipping. Our facility is strategically located near major ports and offers flexible logistics support, giving our customers dependable delivery to key markets. This gives Ecoco Nusantara a distinct edge in reliability, transparency, and long-term partnership.
