COMPANY PROFILE

PT Ecoco Nusantara

Ecoco Nusantara is a trusted and credible supplier and exporter specializing in shisha charcoal briquettes, BBQ charcoal briquettes, and hardwood briquettes. We are empowering global markets through innovative briquette solutions, dedicated to exceeding our partners' expectations on an international scale, all while driving meaningful economic progress for Indonesia.

Beyond our signature coconut shell briquettes, we proudly supply export-grade natural hardwood charcoal. Our selection includes highly sought-after varieties such as tamarind and halaban, all carefully sourced to meet international market demands.

Indonesia is globally recognized for producing the finest coconut charcoal. Leveraging this natural advantage, we guarantee that our products are meticulously processed using only premium-grade raw materials from Sumatra and Sulawesi.
