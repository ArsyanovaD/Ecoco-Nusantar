Charcoal Briquette

What makes coconut shell charcoal briquettes unique is how they burn: a naturally sourced by-product of cutting down trees, it creates a sustainable alternative to traditional wood charcoal. Coconut Shell Charcoal Briquettes burn significantly hotter, last longer, and burn behind any little ash.
