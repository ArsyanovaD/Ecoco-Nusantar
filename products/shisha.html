Coconut Shisha
Charcoal Briquette

What makes coconut shell charcoal briquettes unique is how they burn: a naturally sourced by-product of cutting down trees, it creates a sustainable alternative to traditional wood charcoal. Coconut Shell Charcoal Briquettes burn significantly hotter, last longer, and burn behind any little ash. More importantly, they burn completely clean without smoke, sparks, or harmful chemicals, making them a safe and environmentally friendly choice for shisha, barbeques, and various industrial applications.
